## Description
Day four without sleep… and there's someone standing in the corner of your room.

In 1963, a 17-year-old named Randy Gardner stayed awake for 11 days straight, 264 hours, setting a record so extreme that Guinness World Records later stopped accepting attempts to beat it. So what would actually happen to YOU if you tried it? Kip is about to find out.

We follow every stage: microsleeps behind the wheel, your brain's overnight "wash cycle" shutting down, hunger hormones going rogue, hallucinations, paranoia, memory meltdown, and what happens when you finally sleep. And stay for the twist at the end. It's about you.
